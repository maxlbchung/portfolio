Skill Mutator
May 2026 — Present
Evolves LLM skills by driving a skill evaluator as a tool: parallel edits → combine → critic → evaluate → keep or dump, gated by held-out generalization. Accuracy, token usage, and step count act as the fitness signal.
  • Built during LLM skill mutation research with Prof. Anant Sahai (UC Berkeley).

Reodite — Student Success Tools
May 2026 — Present
General student success tools, all in one platform: course lookup, prerequisite tree visualizations, a drag-and-drop planner for building multi-year schedules that respect prerequisite rules and program requirements, and schedule sharing for whole friend groups. Started as UBC academic tools and keeps covering more ground.
  • Grew out of Reogent — a spatially-aware campus AI ideated at the UBC CIC × AWS Hackathon — merged with ReoditeTools; the tools stayed, the chatbot was dropped.
  • ScheduleSharer folded in: overlays everyone's schedules into one master weekly calendar so friend groups can actually find time to meet.
  • Backed by unified scraped datasets: courses, grades, tuition, building and walking GeoJSON, and study spaces.
