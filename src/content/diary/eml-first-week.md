This marks my first week at UBC's `Emerging Media Lab`!

After this first week, I am super excited for what is coming up next. The office and equipment they have is *super* nice, like I was genuinely shocked. They have more VR headsets than I can count, very roomy desks, and high end PCs. Additionally, the EML team is a very kind, small team, which is perfect.

I was assigned to the `Planetary Health` team, and also plan to work on AI integration for the lab.
